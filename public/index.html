How do I get my cookie?
  1. Open Crunchbase in Chrome while signed in.
  2. Press F12 → Network tab → reload the page.
  3. Click any request to crunchbase.com.
  4. Under Request Headers, copy the entire cookie: value.

Your cookie is your login. Keep it private and never commit it anywhere.
